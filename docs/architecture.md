# Architecture

Map of the code for agents and humans. Terms follow `CONTEXT.md`; gameplay numbers follow `docs/design.md`. World units are **the current Area's unit**: centimetres in the House, Neighborhood and City, 10 m in the Region, 10 km in the Continent, 1,000 km on the Globe, 0.01 AU in the Solar System, 100 ly in the Galaxy, 10 Mly in the Universe (`AREAS[area].unit`, see Units below); the floor is the **XZ plane** (y up). Camera yaw is fixed (camera on +Z looking towards −Z), so "up" on screen is −Z.

**Areas.** A Run goes house → neighborhood → city → region → continent → globe → solar system → galaxy → universe (`AREA_ORDER`, `run.area`). Each area's boss clears it when absorbed (`BossTuning.onDefeat: 'openExit'` → `run.clearArea()`): in an Area with a next one, `run.openExit()` removes the exit obstacle (`layout.exit`: the front door in the hallway's end wall, the road barrier across one end of the neighborhood's main street, the highway on-ramp gate at the east end of the city's main avenue, the barrier across the Region's M1 motorway at the west edge, the launch corridor on the Continent's north coast, drawn as a launch pad with a beam of light up to orbit once open, the escape trajectory on the Globe's north bound over the North Pole, the heliopause gate on the Solar System's north bound, the intergalactic gate on the Galaxy's north bound), sets `houseCleared` / `neighborhoodCleared` / `cityCleared` / `regionCleared` / `continentCleared` / `globeCleared` / `solarSystemCleared` / `galaxyCleared` and emits `exitOpened { area }`; in the last Area (the Universe) it sets `universeCleared`, emits `victory` and ends the Run. When the Bunny touches the open exit, the `exit` System (order 650) calls `run.enterNextArea()` (or the HUD's skip button / Enter calls `run.leaveArea()` from anywhere once `run.canLeaveArea`) → `run.enterArea(next)`, which swaps `run.house` for the next generator's layout (`generateNeighborhood` / `generateCity` / `generateRegion` / `generateContinent` / `generateGlobe` / `generateSolarSystem` / `generateGalaxy` / `generateUniverse`, same `HouseLayout` shape: zones play the part of rooms, houses / buildings and trees are furniture, the hedge / concrete barrier is the walls), resets Debris with the Area's spatial-hash cell, clears Hazards and floor Pickups, re-bases the Bunny into the Area's unit (then the arrival leap, see Units), moves the Bunny to the area spawn (healing 1 heart, none on Hardcore), repopulates and emits `areaEntered`. Size, health, Perks and active Pickups carry over. `RunView` rebuilds the world view, `Game` switches sky/fog/lighting (`Lighting.setArea`) and snaps the camera. Zone types are unique per area (`zoneArea(type)`: house rooms, neighborhood `yard | street | sidewalk | driveway | park`, city `avenue | pavement | plaza | parking | square`, region `farmland | highway | river | town | woods | landfill`, continent `plains | mountains | lakes | rivers | desert | tundra | ruins`, globe `oceans | shelves | landmasses | archipelagos | icecaps`, solar system `corona | inner_orbits | asteroid_belt | outer_orbits | kuiper_belt`, galaxy `galactic_core | bar | inner_arms | dust_lanes | outer_arms | halo`, universe `superclusters | filaments | sheets | voids`); Debris types carry `area` (default `'house'`) and `roomWeight` returns 0 across areas. `AREAS[area].tiers` clamps the spawner's Tier band (house 1–5, neighborhood 5–7, city 7–9, region 10–11, continent 12–13, globe 14–15, solar system 16–17, galaxy 18–19, universe 20–21) and `AREAS[area].growth` scales growth (0.24 in the neighborhood, 0.11 in the city, 0.12 in the region, 0.19 in the continent, 0.20 on the globe, 0.21 in the galaxy, 0.32 in the universe).

**Units** (`docs/story.md`, "Scale without breaking floats"). `AREAS[area].unit` is cm per world unit (1 up to the City, 1,000 in the Region, 1,000,000 in the Continent, 100,000,000 on the Globe, 1.496 × 10¹¹ in the Solar System, 9.461 × 10¹⁹ (100 ly) in the Galaxy, 9.461 × 10²⁴ (10 Mly) in the Universe). The readout ladder (`src/data/units.ts`) ends cm, m, km, Earths, AU, ly, Mly; at Tier 21 (Everything) the HUD size readout and the results screen's max radius read `everything` (`formatSize`). `run.enterArea` calls `bunny.rebase(unit)` before moving the Bunny: radius, `maxRadius`, position and speeds are multiplied by old unit / new unit and `bunnyRebased { factor, unit }` is emitted (not `bunnyGrew`; `BunnyView` rescales its stuck Debris). `bunny.realRadius` (= radius × `bunny.unit`) is what Tiers (`tierForRadius`, `tierProgress`, `growthFactorAt`; thresholds are real cm), readouts and `RunStats.maxRadius` / `finalRadius` use; `AreaStats` radii are in the Area's unit. Everything an Area's generator, Debris and Hazard tunings contain is in its unit, so the Region (Bunny 0.65 → 4.5 units) reuses the House-scale shape of the shared constants; constants that must stay real cm divide by `run.unit` (Hazard director margins, Pickup minimum size, the exit touch margin). `AREA_WORLDS[area].startRadius` (`?area=` starts, `areaStartRadius`) is real cm.

**The size leap.** An Area with `AREAS[area].arrivalRadius` (the Continent, the Globe, the Solar System, the Galaxy and the Universe: 0.85 units) is entered at least that big: after `bunny.rebase`, `run.enterArea` calls `bunny.leapTo(arrival)`, which sets radius, `prevRadius` and `maxRadius` without `bunnyGrew` and emits `bunnyLeapt { radius, previousRadius }` (`BunnyView` rebuilds its stuck Debris, which keeps its size and so drops out of the fur). The Region ends at ≈ 40 m; a real-size re-base would arrive at ≈ 0.004 units (the Globe's ≈ 4,500 km is ≈ 0.003 Solar System units; the Solar System's ≈ 0.045 AU is ≈ 7 × 10⁻⁹ Galaxy units; the Galaxy's ≈ 400 ly is ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁵ Universe units). The arrival radius sits below the Area's first Tier (8.5 km < 10 km), so there is no Tier-up or Perk draft; `beginArea` runs after the leap, so `AreaStats.radiusIn`, Fluff and the Area board count from it. The Run board treats it with a zero band in `SCORE_RADIUS_BANDS` (see Leaderboard). The HUD hides a next Tier that only the leap reaches (`pastNextArrival`: the Region's Tier 11 card shows no Tier 12 goal). Rationale in `docs/design.md`.

**The Continent map** (`continent.ts`, 10 km units, 200 × 150). The edge is sea: `layout.walls` (ids `coast<i>`) are rects of sea along every edge (bays and headlands, a southern gulf, a western bay, cut corners) that `HouseView` draws as ocean through `continentCoast.ts` instead of a wall (`EDGE.continent = 'coast'`; the ground past it is the `ocean` floor). Data for Hazards: rivers are `rivers` zones, one feature per reach in course order (`riverA0`, `riverA1`, …; A drains the lake district east to the coast, B runs from the tundra south through the desert, C drains the lake district north), so a def's `spawnZones: ['rivers']` places a trail-layer on a river; `layout.routes` (`MapRoute`) carries the same courses as axis-aligned polylines (kind `river`, width 3, clipped at the coast) plus glacier lanes (kind `glacier`: straight north–south runs from the north coast across the tundra, clear of obstacles). Mountain ranges are furniture `continent_range` (the cordillera and the south-east range) and `continent_massif` (the northern highlands), a Bunny-wide `obstacleGap` from each other and the sea; `mountainObstacleIds(layout)` returns their obstacle ids (`<furniture id>:body`), which are the only furniture obstacles on the map. The Bunny's fur carries snow-capped ranges (the `mountain_range` Debris geometry) while it is in the Continent (`BunnyView`, from `bunny.unit`).

**The Globe map** (`globe.ts`, 1,000 km units, 480 × 240, `layout.wrapX`). A stylised equirectangular Earth (x is longitude from 180° W, z latitude from 90° N, 1⅓ units per degree, ten times real size so a 6-unit Bunny still has a planet): `SHAPES` holds hand-drawn continent, island and inland-sea outlines in longitude / latitude; `globeSurface(seed, q)` nudges each group by up to `GLOBE.jitter` units (never towards the seam), warps the coasts with periodic noise, cuts the inland seas, adds the polar bands (Arctic sea ice, Antarctica) and rasterises at q cells per unit (cached per seed). `generateGlobe` paints zones from it at 1 cell per unit: one `landmasses` feature per continent (`northAmerica`, `eurasia`, `africa`, …), one `archipelagos` feature per island chain (the islands plus `archipelagoReach` of shallows: `malayArchipelago`, `caribbean`, `hawaii`, `polynesia`, `melanesia`), `shelves` within `shelfWidth` of a coast and the inland seas, `icecaps` (`arctic`, `antarctica`, `greenland`), and `oceans`. Within `seamMargin` of the seam (the date line, in the Pacific) everything is open ocean or polar ice, and there are no obstacles or walls (the poles are the bounds), so nothing straddles the seam. The spawn is the Gulf of Guinea (0° N 0° E). `layout.debrisPlacements` puts `GLOBE.moons` Moons out in the open oceans (the Orbital Sweeper is stunned by charging into a `moon` the Bunny can't absorb yet) and the Great Barrier Reef on Queensland's shelf; `layout.routes` carries three orbit lanes (kind `orbit`, east–west across the whole lap). The view paints the map with `buildGlobeSurface` (`globeTextures.ts`) instead of zone floors. Half a lap (240) stays beyond the widest view of a 6-unit Tier 15 Bunny, so lap moves are off screen. On the Globe the Bunny wears a faint fresnel atmosphere and, from Dust Moon on, the Moon (the `moon` Debris geometry) half-buried in its fur (`BunnyView`).

**The Solar System map** (`solarSystem.ts`, 0.01 AU units, 360 × 360, no wrap). The ecliptic from above, the Sun at the centre (`sunCentre`); zones are rings by distance from it: `corona` (< 14), `inner_orbits` (< 78), `asteroid_belt` (< 102), `outer_orbits` (< 160) and `kuiper_belt` (the rest, out to the bounds). No walls; the bounds rect is the heliopause, painted as the Kuiper belt thinning into interstellar dark with a faint line along the edge. `layout.routes` carries eight orbit rings (kind `orbit`, closed 96-segment polylines, first point repeated last, width 3) at `SOLAR.orbits` (22, 36, 50 (Earth's, empty), 66, 118 (Jupiter), 136, 150, 166). The spawn is on Earth's ring 50 units south of the Sun, so the Sun is up the screen and off it at arrival. `layout.debrisPlacements`: Voyager 1 7 units north-east of the spawn (on screen at arrival, never culled before r ≈ 2), each planet on its ring at a seeded angle (Jupiter on the far side of the Sun, Mars within ≈16–35 units of the spawn, the others clear of Jupiter and the spawn), Pluto in the Kuiper belt, `SOLAR.beltRocks` asteroids across the belt (two Kirkwood gaps) and two camps of Trojans at Jupiter's L4 / L5. The named planets are placed only (empty `rooms`); they block like any Debris too big to absorb, and Jupiter (`lockedUntilTier: 17`) until Tier 17 as well. **Wrap-around** (`src/world/wrap.ts`, `src/systems/wrap.ts`, `HouseView`). A layout with `wrapX` has no east or west edge. World x is unbounded: the Bunny, Debris, Hazards and Pickups keep continuous coordinates as the Bunny circles the planet, so distances between them need no wrapping and the camera never jumps. Static data (rooms, routes, the exit) is stored once in the canonical strip `[bounds.minX, bounds.maxX)` and looked up through `canonicalX` (`roomAt`, floor checks, Hazard movement, collision, the exit); collision and Hazards skip the east–west clamp. The `wrap` System (`WrapSystem`) moves Debris and floor Pickups more than half a lap from the Bunny by whole laps towards it and drops non-boss Hazards that far (the director spawns new ones nearby); the spawner samples round the Bunny without an east or west edge. `HouseView.buildLaps` puts everything but the outer ground into one lap, clones it one width east and west, and moves the three to the camera's lap each frame; anything drawn there must tile across the seam (world-space UVs whose tile sizes divide the width, periodic noise, distance fields padded with the opposite edge). `roomAt` indexes layouts with many rooms (the Globe's coastlines give ≈3,000 zone rects) in a bucket grid. `pnpm sim`'s path planner wraps its columns too.

**Sim / render split.** `Run` and everything it touches (`Bunny`, `DebrisField`, systems, `generateHouse`) never touch the DOM or WebGL, so they run under Vitest and `pnpm sim`. Rendering reads that state each frame (`RunView`, `BunnyView`, `DebrisRenderer`, `HouseView`). The one exception: `run.entityRoot` (a `THREE.Group`) holds visuals owned by Hazards and Pickups; it's harmless headless.

**Debris storage and changes.** `DebrisField` owns private dense per-type buckets, spatial indexing and coalesced change tracking. A Debris's `slot` is its current per-type instance index, not a stable identity: removal moves the bucket's last Debris into the vacated slot. `spawn`, `move`, `remove`, `clear` and `reset` keep storage, the spatial hash and pending changes coherent; moves/removals using dead or foreign handles are ignored. Retained change bookkeeping contains no Debris references: at most `cap` dirty slot numbers and `types.length` changed-type markers, independently of mutation count or renderer presence. `pendingChanges` reports both counts for diagnostics. Headless callers never need to consume changes to stay within that bound.

`consumeChanges()` is a destructive **single-consumer** interface for one active renderer. Each batch supplies a `typeIndex`, read-only current `instances` and unique valid `changedSlots`; `instances.length` is authoritative, including zero for empty changed types. Repeated moves collapse to final state, removal discards the old tail slot, and swap-removal marks the surviving occupant's new slot. These are borrowed views: consume them synchronously before the next field mutation, without mutating their contents or retaining them as snapshots. Attach or replace a renderer using `consumeChanges({ full: true })`, which includes every type and live slot even after earlier consumption. `DebrisRenderer` then consumes incremental batches, writing changed transforms and colors, updating counts/visibility for empty types, and filling **all** current instances when a mesh grows. `clear/reset` discard pending slots and invalidate formerly populated types even across repeated resets before synchronization; `reset` may also replace the spatial hash's cell size. The existing renderer remains attached when an Area resets and repopulates the field.

**Run assembly** (`src/game/assembly.ts`). Every Run is built by `createRun(events, config, rules?)`: `Game.startRun` in the browser, `scripts/sim.ts` and the gameplay integration tests (`src/game/assembly.test.ts`) all use it, so they play by the same rules. It imports no DOM, CSS or WebGL code and runs in plain Node. It:

1. registers the content (every Hazard on import, then Pickups and Perks via the idempotent `registerGameplayContent()`) before `new Run` applies `config.perks`;
2. builds the `Run` (Character stats, Difficulty, Upgrade `modifiers`, starting Perks, `area` start, all seeded from `config.seed`);
3. installs the gameplay rules with `installGameplay(run, rules?)`, in this order: the combat hooks (damage filter first in `bunny.damageFilters`, `combat` System at order 290), Hazard spawning (seeded `HazardDirector` as `run.hazards.director`, plus the `hazardFx` absorb effect System) and Pickup spawning (`run.pickups.director`) and the Character's Ability (`installAbilities`: the `ability` System at order 90).

`installGameplay` is idempotent: calling it again never adds a second filter or System and never replaces a running director (`getHazardDirector(run)` returns the one in use). `rules` turns optional parts off: `{ hazards: false }`, `{ pickups: false }` and/or `{ abilities: false }` (the combat hooks are always installed). `pnpm sim --no-hazards` uses both for pure growth. Browser-only wiring stays out of it: `installRoguelike` (screens, opening the Perk draft modal, Fluff, HUD widgets) and `installHazards` (boss HUD, debug tools) subscribe to `game.events` at boot, and `runStarted` is emitted only after `createRun` returns, so its observers always see a Run whose rules are installed. Headless code should call `createRun` rather than `new Run` unless a test deliberately wants a bare Run (then `installGameplay(run)` adds the rules).

## Game loop

`FixedLoop` (`src/core/loop.ts`) runs `update(1/60)` up to 5 times per frame, then `render(alpha, frameDt)`.

- `Game.update(dt)` calls `run.update(dt)` only while the active screen has `simulate: true` (only `playing` by default). That's how pause works, along with any other modal screen.
- `Run.update(dt)`: `bunny.snapshot()` (for interpolation), `time += dt`, `inputState = input.sample()` (once per tick), then each `System` in ascending `order`:

| order | system | does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90 | `ability` (`AbilitySystem`) | the Character's Ability from `run.inputState` (Decoy, Hunker, Discharge), Grounded countdown |
| 100 | `movement` | input → velocity, Dash, knockback impulse decay, rolling, timers, `stats.tick`; a hunkered Bunny doesn't move |
| 200 | `magnet` | pulls absorbable Debris within `radius × (1 + magnetRadius)` |
| 300 | `hazards` (`HazardManager`) | director, `hazard.update`, contact → absorb or `onContact` |
| 400 | `absorb` | touching Debris with `size ≤ radius × absorbRatio` → `run.absorbDebris` |
| 500 | `collision` | push-out from walls/furniture AABBs (skipped if `2r < clearance`; walls use a squeezed radius inside doorways, `wallRadiusAt`, and walls and furniture a squeezed one when the Bunny is wedged between obstacles on opposite sides, `gapRadiusAt`) and Obstacle Debris circles (Debris up to `radius × TUNING.push.ratio` is shoved instead, `pushDebris`); clamp to bounds |
| 550 | `pickups` (`PickupManager`) | collect floor Pickups, expire timed effects |
| 600 | `growth` | per Tier crossed: queues a Perk draft (`run.perkDraft.queue()`), then emits `tierUp`; records `run.tierTimes` |
| 650 | `exit` | once the area's exit is open, touching it moves the Run to the next area |
| 700 | `spawner` (`DebrisSpawner`) | every 0.25 s: cull far tiny Debris, top up meaningful absorbables on floor that is off screen (`floorPointVisible`), reachable (`reachableBy`) and not cluttered (`clutterFree`) |

Add your own with `run.addSystem({ id, order, update(dt, run) })` (e.g. listen to `runStarted` and add it to `game.run`).

- `Game.render`: `bendCamera` (a lens's lean and roll for the rig), `RunView.render` (Debris instance sync, Bunny view, camera rig, occlusion fade), sun/shadow follow, HUD, `renderer.render`, then `LensingPass.render` (the gravitational-lensing warp, only while a lens exists).

## Core rules (where to tune)

- **Absorb**: `canAbsorb(radius, absorbRatio, size)`, i.e. `size ≤ radius × absorbRatio` (`src/systems/absorb.ts`).
- **Growth**: `growRadius(r, size, mass, growthMultiplier, growthFactor)`, i.e. `cbrt(r³ + growthFactor × growthMultiplier × mass × size³)`. `TUNING.growthFactor` (currently 0.16) × the Tier's `growth` multiplier (`growthFactorAt`, `src/data/tiers.ts`) sets pacing; `pnpm sim` reports Tier times per Character (`--no-hazards` for pure growth, `--god` for Hazards that can't kill).
- **Speed** = `speed stat × TUNING.speedPerRadius × radius` (cm/s). Camera distance = `radius × tier.cameraScale` (smoothed). Fog and the spawner's view radius derive from it.
- **Gating**: furniture with `clearance` lets a Bunny with `2r < clearance` pass underneath (couch 6 cm, armchair 9, bed 24, tables ~40–72). A Bunny that outgrows the gap while underneath is ejected through `obstacle.exitDir` (the furniture front). Doorways (90 cm) are never a gate: the Bunny squeezes through at any size (`bunny.squeeze` drives the view's squash). Corridors aren't either: wedged between obstacles on opposite sides (the hallway walls, a console table and the wall), the fur squeezes down to `TUNING.squeeze.min` (0.4) × radius, so the post-boss Bunny (r ≈ 53–64, wider than the ~106 cm left beside the hallway furniture) still rolls to the front door. Head-on, a gap between two corners only opens from ~1.4 × radius, so small gaps stay closed. Big Debris keeps `clutterSpacing` from its neighbours and almost-absorbable Debris can be pushed, so clutter can't box the Bunny in.
- **Run start**: the Bunny spawns under the front edge of the couch. `DebrisSpawner.populate` places template Debris (chairs, baskets, plants), a dense Speck cluster around the spawn, and a scatter whose Tier band rises with distance from the spawn.

## Bunny stats and modifiers (`src/entities/stats.ts`)

Stats: `speed, acceleration, maxHealth, absorbRatio, growthMultiplier, magnetRadius, dashCooldown, dashSpeed, suctionMultiplier, knockbackResistance, startRadius`, plus roguelike stats `pickupDuration` (Hoarder), `pickupLuck` (Pickup spawn rate), `dodgeChance` (Slippery, max 0.75), `perkChoices` (3; Wide Choice → 4), `perkRerolls` (Reroll Upgrade). Value = base (`BASE_BUNNY_STATS` + Character overrides), then modifiers sorted by `(priority, insertion)`. `add` defaults to priority 100 and `mul` to 200, which gives `(base + Σadd) × Πmul`.

**Caps.** `new StatBlock(base, { caps, capPriority })` (the Run passes the Character's `caps` and `CAP_PRIORITY`, 250): modifiers below 250 (base + Shop + Keepsakes + Perks) are clamped to the cap (`max`, or `min` for the dashCooldown / suctionMultiplier floors), then modifiers at 250 or above apply on top. Timed and Ability-state effects use `TIMED_PRIORITY` (300): Pickups (`pickup:*`), the `hazard:wet` slow and Hunker's planted magnet, so a Sugar Rush keeps full strength and a wet trail still slows Zap below its floor. `STAT_LIMITS` clamp last. `stats.atCap(key)` says whether the value before timed effects has reached the cap; the Perk draft hides a Perk whose `capStats` are all at cap (Thick Fur declares none because it also heals).

```ts
const h = run.bunny.stats.add({ stat: 'speed', op: 'mul', value: 1.5, source: 'pickup:sugar_rush', duration: 8 });
run.bunny.stats.remove(h);               // or
run.bunny.stats.removeSource('perk:aerodynamic');
```

- **Characters**: `src/data/characters.ts` (`stats` overrides base values). `game.nextRunConfig.characterId` / `?character=`.
- **Difficulty**: `RunConfig.difficulty` (`game.nextRunConfig.difficulty`, `?difficulty=`, default from the save's `lastDifficulty`) → `run.difficulty` (a `DifficultyDef` from `src/data/difficulty.ts`). It adds stat modifiers at Run start (`difficultyModifiers`: Hardcore −1 `maxHealth`, ×0.7 `pickupLuck`, source `difficulty:<id>`), sets the hearts healed on reaching a new area (`areaHeal`), scales the Hazard director (budget, spawn interval, grace period) and Hazards: use `BaseHazard.tierSpeed` / `speed(run, cmPerSec)` for any movement and `attackTime(run, seconds)` for telegraphs, warm-ups, cooldowns and summon intervals, so new Hazards follow the Difficulty too. `RunStats.difficulty` feeds the Fluff multiplier and the leaderboard score.
- **Upgrades** (Shop): pass permanent modifiers via `game.nextRunConfig.modifiers` (applied at Run start). `startRadius` is read once at start.
- **Health**: `bunny.damage(amount, source)` respects i-frames (`TUNING.invulnerability`) and `bunny.damageFilters` (return a reduced amount; 0 cancels; used by Bubble Wrap, Slippery and Hunker). Also available: `bunny.heal(n)`, `bunny.knockback(dx, dz, strengthInRadiiPerSec)` (scaled by `knockbackResistance`), `bunny.rooted = seconds` (Lint Roller), `bunny.addVolume(dv)` (shed mass when negative).

## Roguelike layer (Characters, Pickups, Perks, Upgrades, save)

Pickups, Perks, the combat hooks and the Pickup director belong to the Run assembly (see **Run assembly** above). `installRoguelike(game)` (`src/meta/install.ts`) is the browser side: it registers the `characterSelect` / `perkDraft` / `shop` screens, then:
- **Perk draft** on each `tierUp`: `game.go('perkDraft')` if the Run has a pick waiting and the modal isn't already open. The modal only renders `run.perkDraft` and translates input (keys 1–4 / click / tap pick, R rerolls); pointer arming (0.3 s) and pausing (`simulate: false`) are its own. It returns to `playing` once the workflow is complete.

**Perk draft workflow** (`PerkDraft` in `src/perks/draft.ts`, one per Run at `run.perkDraft`, so a new or replaced Run never inherits pending picks). Rule: **one draft per Tier crossed**. `growthSystem` calls `queue()` before each `tierUp`, so several Tier-ups in one update queue several drafts and every `tierUp` listener already sees its draft. State: `pending` (drafts left, the current one included), `offer` (`{ serial, perks }`: `perkChoices` distinct weighted Perks, never an owned unique or maxed one, nor one whose stats are all at the Character cap (`perkCapped`), seeded by the Run's `perk-draft` RNG fork), `rerollsUsed` / `rerollsLeft` (budget from the `perkRerolls` stat). `choose(perkId, serial?)` applies an offered Perk exactly once, consumes one pending draft and prepares the next offer; it returns false and changes nothing for a Perk not on the offer, a stale `serial`, an ended Run or nothing pending. `reroll()` spends one reroll on a new offer avoiding the current one (no pending draft consumed). When nothing is left to offer (every Perk owned or maxed) the remaining drafts are dropped. The balance bot picks headless and immediately in its `tierUp` listener (`run.perkDraft.choose`, random pick from its own seeded RNG).
- **Upgrades → Run**: `game.nextRunConfig.modifiers = getMeta().runModifiers()` at boot and after every purchase (source `upgrade:<id>`, one modifier per Upgrade). `nextRunConfig.characterId` defaults to the last chosen Character.
- **Fluff** on `runEnded`: `getMeta().awardRun(e)` (idempotent per payload; the results screen and leaderboard posting call it too), then `fluffAwarded { amount, total, reason }` is emitted. Formula in `src/meta/fluff.ts` (× the Difficulty's `fluff`).
- **Keepsakes** on `bossDefeated`: `KeepsakeTracker.bossDefeated(defId)` (`src/meta/keepsakes.ts`) rolls the drops (`rollBossDrops`: grade from `gradeForBoss`, type seeded from the Run seed and boss, Hardcore +1, first kill of that boss by that Character offers 3 types and credits the first), credits them to the played Character with `meta.creditKeepsakes` (records the boss in `bossesBeaten`, counts `stats.keepsakesEarned`) and emits `keepsakesDropped`. Runs that `isRankedRun` (`src/ui/leaderboard/posting.ts`) rejects (`?debug`, `?area=`) drop nothing. The results "Kept" section reads `tracker.forResult(e)`. Picking another offered type calls `tracker.choose` (a Hoard swap): in the Keepsake reveal until it closes (`tracker.lock(drop)`), or, for a drop whose reveal never showed because the Run ended first, on the results screen until it exits (`settle`). `keepsakeHud.ts` plays the reward moment: fibres via `PuffParticles.emitInward`, then, once the area-cleared banner is done and the `playing` screen is up (a Perk draft, chapter card or pause goes first), `openKeepsakeReveal` pauses the Run on the `keepsakeReveal` screen (`simulate: false`): boss, drops with their effects, the narrator caption (gated by `storyOn()`), and a first kill's `choiceCard` (click or keys 1–3). Enter / Space / Esc or **Keep rolling** lock the choice (`tracker.lock`) and resume (or open the waiting Perk draft) after a 0.6 s arm delay. A Run-winning boss gets no reveal: the caption plays via `narrate` and results list the drop.
- **Loadout → Run**: `game.nextRunConfig.loadout = meta.lastLoadout(characterId)` at boot and on every save change (so Felting or shedding a tucked-in Keepsake drops it from the Loadout); Character select sets it with `meta.setLastLoadout`. `Run` validates it (`isLoadoutFor`) into `run.loadout`, and `RunStats.loadout` carries it to the leaderboard.
- **Felting / shedding**: `meta.feltCheck(characterId, ref)` (reasons `count | max | ceiling | poor | full`), `meta.felt` (3 → 1 of grade + 1 for `feltCost`, only up to `feltCeiling(bossesBeaten)`), `meta.shed` (+`shedValue` Fluff, not counted in `totalFluffEarned`).
- **Save**: `localStorage['dust-bunny:save:v1']` = `{ version: 1, fluff, upgrades, lastCharacterId, lastDifficulty, settings, stats: { runs, wins, bestRadius, bestTier, totalFluffEarned, keepsakesEarned, feltings }, characters: { [characterId]: { hoard: { "type:grade": count (1–99) }, bossesBeaten: [bossId] } }, lastLoadout: { [characterId]: [{ type, grade }] }, universes }`. `characters`, `lastLoadout`, `keepsakesEarned`, `feltings` and `universes` are additive (no version bump); `lastLoadout` is repaired against the Hoard when read. `parseSave` never throws: it migrates unversioned data, sanitises fields one by one, and backs corrupt JSON up to `…:corrupt`. Settings: `getMeta().settings` / `getMeta().updateSettings(patch)` (primitive values only). Listen with `getMeta().store.onChange(cb)`.
- **Universes**: `awardRun` also counts a `victory` in the last Area of `AREA_ORDER` (`endsUniverse(e)`, the Universe) in the save's top-level `universes`, so the count is current before the ending and results screens render; `RunAward.universes` is the count after the Run. The title screen's `enter()` swaps the tagline to `Universe N · Roll. Absorb. Grow.` (N = `universes` + 1) once it is at least 1, and the results screen lists a **Universes** stat (`N · +1` on the Run that ended one) once it is at least 1.
- **Character look**: `CharacterDef.look = { color, tipColor, furLength, fluffiness, eyeStyle: 'round' | 'sleepy' | 'sparkly', glow? }`. `BunnyView` currently applies only `color`.
- **Events added**: `shieldBlocked`, `damageDodged`, `bunnyRevived`, `hazardStunned`, `fluffAwarded`, `upgradePurchased`, `keepsakesDropped`.
- **Screens**: `underTheCouch` (params `{ back?, characterId? }`; key U from Character select and results), `keepsakeReveal` (params `KeepsakeRevealParams`) and the results `kept` section are registered by `installRoguelike`.

**Hooks other modules must respect** (fields on `Bunny` / `Hazard`):

| Hook | Set by | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `boss.groundedFor`, `boss.stunCause` | Discharge, Abilities | A Grounded boss can't be Discharged. `stunCause` is what stunned the boss last (`wall`, `spiky`, `decoy`, `hunker`, `discharge`). |

## Abilities

Every Character has an Ability from the first Run (`CharacterDef.ability`, numbers in `src/data/abilities.ts`). `installAbilities(run)` (from `installGameplay`) adds one `AbilitySystem` (order 90) whose controller is the Character's `DecoyAbility`, `HunkerAbility` or `DischargeAbility`. Input: `ability` (edge) and `abilityHeld` on `InputState`, from E, the right mouse button (context menu suppressed on the canvas) and the touch button. Ranges are in Bunny radii (r).

- **Decoy** (Lint, tap): a still lint ball (0.8 r) for 4 s, cooldown 16 s from when it ends. It is the Run's Lure (`setLure`): aiming Hazards target it while it is within their range (bosses: `chargeTrigger`; Broom: aggro range; Spray Bottle family: range; Upright Vacuum family: cone reach). Vacuum suction drags it in. A non-ranged Hazard touching it is clogged (stunned 1.5 s) and pops it; a spray or blast pops it without a clog.
- **Hunker** (Tumble, hold): planted for up to 10 s (`bunny.hunkered`), cooldown 4 s + 1 s per second held; can't start while stuck to a Lint Roller. A boss hunting or charging into it is stunned for its `stunTime` and chipped at once; other Hazards recoil or run `onPlanted`.
- **Discharge** (Zap, tap when full): the Static meter fills over 40 r rolled above 50% of max speed (Dash counts double) and drains 10%/s below 20%. When full, the nearest Hazard within 4 r of the Bunny's surface gets a spark marker (intangible Hazards, absorbable Hazards, Grounded bosses and bosses inside the limit are skipped). A non-boss is destroyed into 4 scrap pieces (`scatterDebris`, 0.4 r); a boss is stunned 1.5 s without a chip, then Grounded for 10 s.
- **Boss limit**: `AbilitySystem.stunBoss` allows one Ability stun per boss every 6 s (`ABILITY_RULES.bossStunInterval`). Wall crashes and Spiky Dash don't count.

**Extending.** Read every number from `getAbilitySystem(run).params` (`AbilityParams`, built once per Run by `resolveAbilityParams`). To change them per Run, register `addAbilityParamsModifier((params, run) => …)` at import time. Effects that need code use the events above or `getAbilitySystem(run).hooks` (`decoyEnded`, `plantedTouched`, `bossStunned`, `discharged`), which get object references; each hook holds one callback, and the Keepsake effects own the first three.

**Keepsake effects** (values in `src/data/keepsakes.ts`, the table in `docs/design.md`). `run.loadout` (0–2 Keepsakes, copies add up through `loadoutValue`) reaches the Run two ways:
- **Numbers** (`keepsakeParams.ts`): one params modifier calls `applyLoadout(params, run.loadout)`, which adds the Loadout to `decoy.duration / cooldown / clogStun / bossClogStun / lureRangeMul / wandererLureRange`, `hunker.cooldownPerSecondHeld / bossStunBonus / plantedMagnetBonus` and `discharge.fillRate / range / extraTargets / chargedMagnetBonus / fullSuctionMul`, then applies `KEEPSAKE_FLOORS` (Decoy cooldown ≥ its duration; Hunker ≥ 0.2 s per second held). Character select's Ability card shows `abilityParamsFor(loadout)` (`src/ui/keepsakeUi.ts`), the same resolver, so the card and the Run agree. Snowball's planted magnet is added by Hunker at `TIMED_PRIORITY` (above the cap); Static Cling and Grounding are added by Discharge at priority 150 (below the cap).
- **Code** (`keepsakeEffects.ts`, installed by `installAbilities` right after the Ability System, so it runs headless too): Reclaim (`decoyEnded`, not on an Area change) scatters lint fibre sized with `planShed`, so re-absorbing all of it gives back its share of the Bunny's volume; Bristle Back (`plantedTouched`, non-boss) stuns the Hazard and slides it back over 0.2 s with `moveCircle` (Hazards with `onPlanted` or `ranged` are only stunned); Dead Weight (`bossStunned` with cause `hunker`) sets `boss.chipBonus = { hold, pieces }`, which the chip reads (the stun lasts `0.6 + hold` s after it and `pieces` more parts fall; every new stun clears it); Thick Undercoat adds `keepsake:thick_undercoat` stat modifiers (`knockbackResistance` add, `suctionMultiplier` × (1 − total)) at the default priorities, below the cap.

## Story

The narrator (`docs/story.md`) is dressing: it changes no gameplay number and every piece is skippable. Lines live in `src/data/story.ts` (`STORY`): intro panels, Tier-up lines keyed by Tier id, one chapter per Area (`title`, `entered`, `cleared`, `boss`) and one-off lines keyed by Debris type id. A line is a caption or an array of captions shown in turn; each caption is at most 140 characters, with no exclamation marks (`src/data/story.test.ts`). Each beat lists variants; `pickLine(seed, beat)` picks one from the Run seed and the beat alone, so the choice is deterministic and independent of what else was said. The chapter number is the Area's position in `AREA_ORDER`.

`narrate(run, beat)` (`src/story/index.ts`) returns null when the story is off: the `storyCaptions` setting, or `?autostart` (`installStory(game, { available: false })`). The UI that already shows each moment hosts its line:

| Beat | Host |
| --- | --- |
| Tier-up | Perk draft header (`ui/screens/perkDraft.ts`); with no draft waiting, a caption on the Tier-up banner (`ui/hud.ts`) |
| Boss arrives | Boss banner: boss name, "Boss incoming", caption (`ui/hud.ts`) |
| Area cleared | Caption on the exit-opened banner, replacing the directions (`ui/areaHud.ts`) |
| Area entered | `chapter` screen (`simulate: false`, Space / Enter / tap cuts it short), then play or the waiting Perk draft resumes, then the arrival banner (`showChapterCard` from `ui/areaHud.ts`) |
| One-off Debris | Banner with a caption the first time per Run (`story/install.ts`) |
| Run ends | Results line: "Swept up in Chapter N · title", or the last Area's cleared line on a win (`ui/screens/results.ts`) |

`hud.banner(title, { caption: { parts, holds } })` shows a caption under the subtitle, part after part, each for its reading time at 100 WPM (`captionHold`; `storyCaption` builds it, `captionDuration` sums it). While a caption plays, later banners queue behind it rather than replace it (`hud.bannerBusy`, `hud.bannerLeft` counts the queue); the boss Stunned / Chipped news goes to a toast instead. The intro (`intro` screen, static cards over dust motes) plays on the first Play while `introSeen` is false (`playFromMenu`) and from Settings → Story → Replay intro. All three story screens use `storyCards` (`play(panels, done, onPanel)`; it exposes the panel `index`, `elapsed` and `hold`, and plays panels without a caption for exactly their `hold`).

The ending (`ending` screen, `src/story/endingScreen.ts`, `simulate: false`) plays between a victory in the last Area and the results. Game routes there with `afterRunEnded(reason, run.area, storyAvailable())` (`src/story/ending.ts`): a `victory` in `ENDING_AREA` (`'universe'`) while the story is available (not `?autostart`) goes to `ending` 0.8 s later (replacing the victory hold), then `results` with the same `runEnded` payload. The Story captions setting only hides the captions. Its ten shots are `STORY.ending` (`shot`, optional `caption`, `hold`, `cue`); the shot after the Bang is the first that Space / Enter / tap can skip (`canSkipEnding`, `ENDING_SKIPPABLE_FROM`). While it is up, `game.ending` holds an `EndingScene` (`src/fx/ending.ts`: its own Scene and camera on the game's renderer, drawn instead of the Run): a BunnyView of its own in the black-hole form over a still stand-in Bunny, one Points draw of motes for the Bang, swirl and disc, a shader planet (the game's only real sphere) and a procedural low-poly shore with a biped built from boxes, a fire, a stick and a speck-sized dust bunny. `update` returns the veil (black fades, the Bang's white frame) that the screen draws; "The End.", "?" and "…" are DOM text. Reduced motion slows the pushes and the burst and turns the white frame into a soft fade. Moments inside a shot (rock strikes, the drop, the walk, the staple) are `ENDING_BEATS`, shared by the picture and `EndingAudio` (held note with the music hushed, silence, thump and shimmer, the Tier 1 theme, clacks and crackle, footsteps (`sfx.footstepsReceding`), room tone, staple; leaving the screen fades what still sounds).

## Hazards

Importing `src/hazards/content.ts` registers every Hazard; the Run assembly imports it and installs the spawn director plus the absorb effect System on each Run. `installHazards(game)` (`src/hazards/install.ts`, called from `main.ts`) adds only the browser side: the boss HUD and the `?debug` tools.

- **Hazards** (`BaseHazard` in `base.ts`): Roomba (wander, wall bounce, suction ring), Lint Roller (lane back and forth, sticks the Bunny ~1 s and sheds mass), Broom (red arc telegraph, then sweep with big knockback), Spray Bottle (turret: aim lane telegraph, burst of droplets), Mop (patrol loop, fading wet trail that slows via a `hazard:wet` speed modifier), Upright Vacuum (amber cone warm-up, then a suction cone), Robo-Vac 9000 (boss) and its mini Roombas. `object3d` is a Group at the world origin; `body` holds the model at the Hazard position, decals are separate world-space meshes. A green ring marks a Hazard the Bunny can absorb.
- **Outdoor Hazards** reuse those classes with their own tuning (`RoombaTuning`, `LaneTuning`, `TurretTuning`, `AirConeTuning`, `BossTuning` in `src/data/hazards.ts`; outdoor per-Tier values come from `outdoorTiers(t5, t6, t7)`). Robotic Mower (`Roomba`, size 55, weak blade pull, spins `blade`). Lawn Mower (`LintRoller` lane, size 65; no sticking: a hit knocks the Bunny off the lane, sheds mass and pauses the mower). Leaf Blower (`UprightVacuum` in `blow` mode, size 45: amber warm-up, then a blast that pushes the Bunny away from the nozzle (stronger up close) and blows Debris ≤ 20 cm along; only starts on screen). Sprinkler (`SprayBottle`, size 45: only the `head` node turns, it sweeps idly out of range, and a water hit adds a 1.5 s `hazard:wet` slow).
- **City Hazards** do the same with `cityTiers(t7, t8, t9)` and `MopTuning`. Their Blender models are real-size, so every tuning has a `modelSize` (model scale = size / modelSize; 1 for the house and neighborhood ones). Floor Scrubber (`Roomba`, size 190, weak scrub-brush pull, spins `brush`). Pressure Washer (`SprayBottle`, size 170: `head` turns, a burst of five fast water jets, a hit soaks like the Sprinkler; only fires on screen). Litter Vacuum (`UprightVacuum` in suction mode, size 220, contact sheds mass). Street Flusher (`Mop`, size 300, from Tier 8: patrols waypoints within `patrolRange` of its spawn in its zone, leaves a wide wet trail, knockback on contact).
- **Region Hazards** use `regionTiers(t10, t11)` and Region units (1 = 10 m) for every length and speed; `modelSize` is the real-size model's footprint radius in cm. Landfill Compactor (`Roomba`, size 1.05, spins `beacon`), Crop Duster (`SprayBottle`, size 1.25: the whole plane turns, `propeller` spins about Z via `TurretTuning.spinNode`, pesticide soaks), River Barge (`Mop`, size 1.5; its def's `spawnZones: ['river']` makes the director place it in `river` zones when the map has one), Snowplow (`LintRoller` lane, size 1.7, `LaneTuning.modelSize`) from Tier 10; Water Bomber (`UprightVacuum` in `blow` mode, size 2.3; `AirConeTuning.wetSeconds` soaks while the blast reaches the Bunny) and Freight Train (lane, size 2.45) from Tier 11. Shared Hazard code keeps its absolute cm margins in real cm by dividing by `run.unit` (director ring / despawn / debug and boss fallbacks, back-off speed, boss summon gap, Mop inset, lane probe step).
- **Collision**: `moveCircle` resolves Hazards against walls and furniture whose `clearance` is at most the Hazard's height. A Bunny hiding under furniture the Hazard cannot enter (`bunnySheltered`, e.g. under the couch) gets no contact or suction.
- **Damage** always goes through `bunny.damage()`; knockback, stick and shedding only happen when it returns true. Knockback is a distance: `max(knockbackCm, knockbackRadii × radius)`. Suction pulls at a fraction of the Bunny's max speed × falloff × `suctionMultiplier` (weaker during i-frames). Hazards honour `hiddenFromHazards` (no aiming, chasing or suction), `stunnedFor` (no movement or attacks), `aimTarget` (Lint's Decoy) and `bunny.hunkered` (see the hooks table). After landing a hit, Roomba / Mop / Upright Vacuum set `recoilFor` (`HAZARD_RECOIL`): they back off and deal no contact damage, so they can't pin the Bunny against a wall; the vacuum also cuts out. Ranged attacks (Spray Bottle shots, boss charges) only start while the Hazard is on screen (`onScreen`).
- **Shedding** (`shed.ts`): removes a fraction of r³ and scatters Debris around the Bunny sized so that re-absorbing every piece gives the volume back.
- **Director** (`director.ts`): `DIRECTOR.tiers` in `src/data/hazards.ts` sets per Tier an alive budget and spawn interval that ramp with Tier progress, plus weighted types with caps. 12 s grace at Run start. Spawns in a ring just outside the view, on a spot clear for the def's `footprint`; despawns Hazards that stay far away for 4 s or are out of view and smaller than radius × 0.12. Hazard speeds are per Tier and ramp up to +15 % within a Tier. The Run's Difficulty multiplies the budget, spawn interval, grace period and Hazard speeds (`src/data/difficulty.ts`). In the neighborhood it reads `NEIGHBORHOOD_DIRECTOR.tiers` (Tiers 5–7, 10 s grace after arriving): Robotic Mower, Sprinkler and Leaf Blower from Tier 5, Lawn Mower from Tier 6. In the city, `CITY_DIRECTOR.tiers` (Tiers 7–9): Floor Scrubber, Pressure Washer and Litter Vacuum from Tier 7, Street Flusher from Tier 8. `DIRECTOR_EXCLUDE` keeps bosses and their summons (`mini_roomba`, `mini_mower`, `mini_scrubber`) out of the random picks and the budget.
- **Bosses** (`robovac.ts`, one class driven by `BossTuning`): 3 s after reaching Tier 5 the Robo-Vac 9000 arrives (`bossSpawned`, HUD banner and status line). It hunts with mild suction, telegraphs charges with a red lane, summons mini Roombas, and is stunned when a charge hits a wall. Touching it while stunned (or after a Spiky Dash stun) chips it to `size × 0.88` and knocks absorbable parts loose. Size 36 needs radius ≈ 51 to absorb; after one chip ≈ 45, after two ≈ 40. Absorbing it emits `bossDefeated` and opens the front door (`run.openExit()`).
- **Street Sweeper** (neighborhood boss, `STREET_SWEEPER`): arrives 4 s after Tier 7. Same state machine at street scale (size 150, contact radius 160); houses, trees and the hedge stun its charges, chips are `× 0.85`, and it summons mini mowers. It needs radius ≈ 214 to absorb, ≈ 182 after one chip and ≈ 155 after two (Tier 7 starts at 160). Absorbing it kills its minis, emits `bossDefeated` and opens the road barrier to the city.
- **Garbage Truck** (city boss, `GARBAGE_TRUCK`): arrives 4 s after Tier 9. Size 560, contact radius 590 (model × 1.3); its compactor sucks the Bunny in while it hunts, buildings and the concrete barrier stun its charges, chips are `× 0.85`, and it summons mini scrubbers. It needs radius ≈ 800 to absorb, ≈ 680 after one chip and ≈ 578 after two (Tier 9 starts at 600). Chip pieces are the closest-sized Debris valid at the Bunny's Tier (`pickShedType`), so city Debris (Tiers 7–9) is used once it exists; otherwise the closest size from any area. Absorbing it kills its minis, emits `bossDefeated` and opens the highway on-ramp to the region.
- **The Incinerator** (region boss, `INCINERATOR`): arrives 4 s after Tier 11. Size 2.8 units, contact radius 2.95; feed-conveyor suction while it hunts, towns and the map edge stun its charges, chips are `× 0.85`, summons mini compactors. It needs radius ≈ 4 to absorb, ≈ 3.4 after one chip and ≈ 2.9 after two (Tier 11 starts at 3). ## How to…

**Add a Debris type.** Append to `DEBRIS_TYPES` in `src/data/debris.ts` (or `registerDebrisType()` before a Run starts) with `id, name, size (radius cm), mass, colors, recipe, rooms, tiers`. Recipe parts (`box | sphere | cylinder | cone | torus | capsule | blob`) are in normalised units: `size` maps to 1 and y = 0 is the floor. Final colour is part `color` × an instance colour picked from `colors`. Each type gets one `InstancedMesh`. `tiers: [min, max]` controls when the spawner produces it (one Tier early if already absorbable), and `rooms` holds per-room weights (`any` is the fallback). For a fixed placement (like chairs), call `ctx.placeDebris` in a room template. A type whose id (or `model`) matches a loaded model renders the Blender prop (`debrisGeometry.ts`); optional `material`: `roughness`, `metalness`, `emissive` / `emissiveIntensity` (a uniform glow, recipes), `glow` (GLB props: each part glows with its own Blender emission, baked per vertex as an `emission` attribute, × `glow`; baking a prop into one vertex-coloured mesh otherwise drops per-part emission; the Galaxy's stars and nebulae use it) and `tintAll` (the instance colour multiplies every part of the prop and its glow, for one prop reused at several sizes).

**Add a Hazard.** Subclass `BaseHazard` (implement `tick(dt, run)` and `onContact(run)`), register a def with `hazardRegistry.register({ id, name, footprint, mass, modelId, create })`, and import the file from `content.ts`. The def is the one place that knows how to build the Hazard and where it fits: `footprint` (`{ radius, height }`, checked with `hazardFloorFree`) is read by director spawns, boss arrivals, boss summons and `debugTools.spawnHazard`. Use `tuningFootprint(cfg)` (the tuning's `size` × `height`) unless the Hazard needs a deliberate envelope (the Broom reserves half its sweep, bosses their contact radius). A Hazard that is no body (the Expansion) sets `intangible`: it never touches the Bunny or the Decoy, and Abilities and Spiky Dash skip it. Then decide when it spawns in the balance data in `src/data/hazards.ts`: a weighted, capped entry in `DIRECTOR.tiers` (house), `NEIGHBORHOOD_DIRECTOR.tiers` (outdoor) or `CITY_DIRECTOR.tiers` (city); or, for a boss or a summon, `AREA_BOSSES` / a `BossTuning.miniId` plus `DIRECTOR_EXCLUDE`. These tables are the only selection data (defs carry no weights, caps or first Tier). Finally add it to the `TUNING` (or `ENVELOPES`) table in `src/hazards/placement.test.ts`, which checks every registered footprint against its tuning. A variant of an existing behaviour is usually just a new tuning object passed to the existing class (see the outdoor Hazards). `HazardManager` absorbs a Hazard when `size ≤ radius × absorbRatio` (`run.absorbMass` with the def's `mass`, `hazardAbsorbed`, `onAbsorbed`); otherwise it calls `onContact`.

**Add a Pickup.** Add data to `src/data/pickups.ts` and a def to `PICKUP_DEFS` in `src/pickups/pickups.ts` (`{ id, name, icon, description, color, duration?, spawnWeight, apply(run, duration) }`). `apply` may return an undo callback that runs on expiry; collecting an active timed Pickup undoes and re-applies it with a fresh timer. Durations are scaled by the `pickupDuration` stat (Hoarder). The director spawns from all registered Pickups by `spawnWeight`; `run.pickups.spawn(defId, x, z)` spawns one directly.

**Add a Perk.** Add data to `src/data/perks.ts` and a def to `PERK_DEFS` in `src/perks/perks.ts` (`{ id, name, icon, description, unique?, maxStacks?, weight?, apply(run, stacks) }`). Apply with `run.applyPerk(id)`. `availablePerks(run)` filters out unique or maxed Perks. Typical apply: `run.bunny.stats.add({ stat, op, value, source: 'perk:<id>' })`.

**Add an Upgrade.** Append to `UPGRADES` in `src/data/upgrades.ts` (`stat`, `op`, `perLevel`, `maxLevel`, `baseCost`, `costGrowth`). The Shop lists it and `upgradeModifiers` applies it.

**Add a screen.** Screens are the game state machine:

```ts
registerScreen({
  id: 'perkDraft', simulate: false, hud: true,
  create(game) { const el = h('div', { className: 'panel' }); return { el, enter(params) {…}, exit() {…}, onKey(e) { return false; } }; },
});
game.go('perkDraft', params);   // Run stops simulating; `paused` is emitted with reason 'perkDraft'
```

Re-registering an existing id (`title`, `playing`, `paused`, `results`) replaces the placeholder, but only if you do it before that screen is first shown. Screen DOM is created lazily. `Esc`/`P` handling lives in each screen's `onKey`. `game.startRun(config?)`, `game.quitRun()`, `game.disposeRun()`, and `game.nextRunConfig` exist for character select and the Shop.

**Add a HUD widget.** `game.hud.addWidget({ id, el, slot, update(game, dt) })` with slot `top-left | top-right | top-center | bottom-left | bottom-right | bottom-center | center | status | perks` (`status` is the row under the Tier card, `perks` the row under the top-right counters). Remove one with `removeWidget(id)`. Also: `game.hud.toast(text, { key, tone })` (batched, rate-limited), `game.hud.banner(title, { subtitle, variant: 'tier' | 'boss' | 'good' | 'info', duration })`, `game.hud.flash(widgetId)`. Active Pickups and taken Perks render automatically from `PickupDef.icon` / `PerkDef.icon`; `registerHudIcon(kind, id, { glyph, svg, color, label })` overrides the art.

**Add UI.** Build screens from `src/ui/components.ts` (or the `.panel`, `.btn .btn-primary|secondary|ghost`, `.menu`, `.menu-grid`, `.field` classes) and the CSS custom properties in `src/styles.css`. Call `handleMenuKeys(e, el)` from `onKey` for arrow-key navigation. Results content goes in `registerResultsSection({ id, slot: 'fluff' | 'leaderboard' | 'extra', render(el, result, game) })`. Settings: `settings.value`, `settings.update(patch)`, `settings.subscribe(fn)` from `src/ui/settings.ts`. Sounds: `getAudio()?.sfx.pickup()` etc.; buttons in `#ui` get hover/click sounds automatically.

**Override a model with a `.glb`.** Put the file in `public/models/` and list it in `public/models/manifest.json`:

```json
{ "couch": { "file": "couch.glb", "fit": "xz" }, "roomba": { "file": "roomba.glb", "fit": "none", "collapse": true } }
```

`loadModelOverrides()` runs at boot (async, 8 s timeout, never throws). `createModel(id, { w, d, h, seed })` returns a clone of the GLB. By default the clone is fitted to the requested footprint box (origin at footprint centre, y = 0 floor, front faces +Z). `fit: "xz"` stretches only X/Z and keeps the authored height (furniture, so clearances stay exact). With `fit: "none"` only `scale` is applied, and a model reaching below y = 0 is lifted onto the floor (the broom's handle-top origin ends up on the floor below the pivot). `collapse: true` merges each node's primitives into one vertex-coloured mesh; named nodes (`side_brush`, `drum`, `brush_l`/`brush_r`) survive cloning. All Blender models are listed in the manifest (see `docs/models.md`). With no override, it falls back to the procedural factory (`registerModel(id, factory)`). Furniture ids: `couch, armchair, coffee_table, tv_stand, bookshelf, dining_table, kitchen_counter, stove, fridge, bed, nightstand, dresser, desk, bathtub, toilet, sink_cabinet, console_table, shoe_rack`. Debris types whose id matches a loaded model (shoe, sock, toy_car, book, tv_remote, chair, laundry_basket; in the Continent forest_patch, lake, city_ruins, mesa, iceberg, mountain_range; on the Globe satellite, cloud_swirl, rocket_stage, space_junk, atoll, island, ocean_patch, archipelago, moon, landmass, plus types that name one in `model`, e.g. `big_island` → island, `continent_whole` → landmass) use the baked GLB in their InstancedMesh, scaled to the type's size; others use recipes.

**Add input.** Implement `InputSource.sample(): { moveX, moveZ, dash, ability?, abilityHeld? }` (world XZ, |move| ≤ 1, dash and ability edge-triggered, abilityHeld while held) and call `game.input.add(source)`. `screenToWorld(right, up, TUNING.camera.yaw)` converts a screen-relative stick vector. Mouse steering ignores `pointerType === 'touch'`, leaving touch to a joystick source.

**Add an area.** Add the id to `AreaId` and `AREA_ORDER` (`src/world/types.ts`) plus its zone types, a generator returning a `HouseLayout` in the Area's unit (with an `exit` if another area follows; give the previous last Area its `exit` too; set `wrapX` for a map that wraps east–west, see Wrap-around), an `AREA_WORLDS` entry in `run.ts`, an `AREAS` entry (`unit`, `readout`, `fluffPerUnit`, Tier band, growth, and `arrivalRadius` if the previous Area ends far below ~1 of the new unit), an `AREA_DIRECTORS` table and `AREA_BOSSES` entry in `src/data/hazards.ts`, sky/light in `game.ts` / `lighting.ts`, its exit compass and skip button text in `ui/areaHud.ts`, and its chapter in `STORY.areas` (title plus arrival, boss and cleared lines; `src/data/story.ts` doesn't compile without it) with `STORY.tiers` lines for its new Tiers (`src/data/story.test.ts` checks both). New Tiers go in `src/data/tiers.ts` in real cm. For the leaderboards mirror it in `src/shared/score.ts` (`SCORE_AREAS`, `SCORE_TIER_MIN_RADII`, `AREA_BOSS_TIER`, `SCORE_AREA_UNIT`, `SCORE_ARRIVAL_RADIUS`, `AREA_SCORE.perRadiusUnit` / `parSec`, a `SCORE_RADIUS_BANDS` band for a new unit, plus a zero band over any arrival leap) and `src/shared/leaderboard.ts` (`BOUNDS.areaGrowthPerSec` / `areaSlack`, `AREA_RADIUS`); `src/shared/score.test.ts` checks the mirrors. Also: the Run's cleared flag and entry time (`<area>Cleared` / `<area>At` in `run.ts`, `isAreaCleared`, `RunStats`), the Fluff multiplier for a Run that cleared the previous Area (`meta/fluff.ts`, and its results line), the boss's Keepsake grade (`GRADES[].bossId` in `src/data/keepsakes.ts`, `dropCaption`), `BOARD_NAMES` (`posting.ts`) and the tab rows (`leaderboard.css`), the results screen's cleared rows and died lines, the menu's how-to-play rules, the debug `enter<Area>()` tool, `HAZARD_MODEL_SIZES` plus a fallback factory for each new Hazard model id (`src/hazards/models.ts`), and the Area list in `src/hazards/placement.test.ts`.

**Add a room / furniture.** Furniture defs go in `src/world/furniture.ts` (`clearance` + `legs` for things you can roll under). Room templates go in `src/world/rooms.ts` and use `FurnishContext` (`placeAgainstWall`, `place`, `placeDebris`, `addRug`, `setSpawn`), which rejects overlaps with doorways and other furniture.

## Leaderboard

Ten boards: the **Run board** (`run`, table `leaderboard_scores`, whole Runs) and one **Area board** per Area (`house`, `neighborhood`, `city`, `region`, `continent`, `globe`, `solar_system`, `galaxy`, `universe`, table `area_scores`; the tabs wrap into Full run on its own row, then three rows of three Areas; Area board sizes read out on the Area's ladder, `AREAS[area].readout`). `GET|POST /api/leaderboard?board=<id>` (default `run`).

- **Area entries.** `Run` records each Area it plays (`RunStats.areas: AreaStats[]`: entry time, time in the Area, radius in, peak radius, Debris absorbed, `cleared`). An Area gets an entry once it is cleared: leaving it after its boss (`areaCompleted`), or the Run ending there after the boss (dying in the house after the front door opened, or winning in the City). Score: `computeAreaScore` in `src/shared/score.ts` (growth in the Area, Debris, a clear bonus and seconds under the Area's par). Area board radii are in the Area's unit (`SCORE_AREA_UNIT`; a Region entry of 4.1 is 41 m, a Continent entry of 4.1 is 41 km, a Globe entry of 4.1 is 4,100 km, a Solar System entry of 4.1 is 0.041 AU, a Galaxy entry of 4.1 is 410 ly, a Universe entry of 4.1 is 41 Mly) and its score counts `AREA_SCORE.perRadiusUnit[area]` per unit grown (100 per cm up to the City, 10,000 per Region, Continent, Globe, Solar System, Galaxy or Universe unit; the Continent, the Globe, the Solar System, the Galaxy and the Universe count from the radius after the arrival leap, and their entry bound is that radius, `SCORE_ARRIVAL_RADIUS`). The server checks the entry radius (a later Area needs the previous boss's Tier), the boss Tier, a per-Area radius cap and that the growth fits the time (`areaRadiusAllowed`: entry radius × `areaEntryGrowth` + `areaSlack[area]` + `areaGrowthPerSec[area]` × seconds; the City allows 5 cm/s, the Region 0.05 units/s, the Continent, the Globe and the Solar System 0.08, the Galaxy 0.1, the Universe 0.2, the others 2). The Run board takes the real max radius (cm, up to 300 Mly) and scores it on a progress scale (`progressRadius`: a cm counts 1 up to 10 m, then 0.1 up to 50 m, so one Region unit is worth 100; nothing from 50 m to the Continent's arrival radius of 8.5 km, the leap; then 1e-4, so one Continent unit is worth 100 too; nothing from 60 km to the Globe's arrival radius of 850 km, the second leap; then 1e-6, so one Globe unit is worth 100; nothing from 6,000 km to the Solar System's arrival radius of 0.0085 AU, the third leap; then 100 per 0.01 AU; nothing from 0.06 AU to the Galaxy's arrival radius of 85 ly, the fourth leap; then 100 per 100 ly; nothing from 600 ly to the Universe's arrival radius of 8.5 Mly, the fifth leap; then 100 per 10 Mly up to 30 Mly (Tier 21) and 10 per 10 Mly past it, since the Expansion fight feeds the Bunny whole filaments); its growth check is `progressRadius(maxRadius) ≤ runRadiusAllowed(seconds)` (2.8 per second plus 2.5), and a victory needs Tier 9 or more (clients from before the Region won in the City).
- **Posting** (`src/ui/leaderboard/posting.ts`, installed from `main.ts`). On `runStarted` it opens a `RunPosts` with a random `runId`; `areaCompleted` adds an Area entry, `runEnded` adds the Run entry. Signed in, each Area posts at once (HUD toast with the rank), the Run posts when it ends, and anything still waiting posts on sign-in. Signed-in posts never fall back to the local board: a failure stays retryable (Retry on the results screen). Anonymous, entries wait for the results screen's single submit. Runs played with `?debug` or started with `?area=` are not ranked (entries show as not eligible). The results screen reads the same `RunPosts` through `posting.forResult(e)`.
- **Universes.** Run board submissions carry an optional `universes` (the player's count after the Run, from `awardRun`; `RunPosts` gets it from `installLeaderboardPosting`'s `universes` option and `submissionFromRun` sends it): an integer 0–100,000, at least 1 on a victory (a victory ends a Universe), absent or null from older clients (stored as NULL). It is stored in `leaderboard_scores.universes integer` and returned in GET; the panel shows a 🌀N badge in the Tier cell for entries with 1 or more, and the local board keeps it.
- **Loadout.** Run and Area submissions carry `loadout: [{ type, grade }]` (0–2 of the Character's own types, grades 1–10, checked against the mirror in `src/shared/keepsakes.ts`), stored in `loadout jsonb` columns (NULL for older clients, which are still accepted) and returned in GET; the panel shows two grade-coloured pips beside the Character.
- **Sim check.** `pnpm sim --validate` builds each Run's Run board and Area board submissions with `submissionFromRun` / `areaSubmission`, runs them through `validateSubmission` / `validateAreaSubmission`, prints every rejection (exit code 1) and the closest a Run came to the growth limits (radius reached ÷ radius allowed for the time). Use it with `--loadout=max` after changing anything that adds burst growth (Keepsakes, boss parts, scrap). `src/game/leaderboardSim.test.ts` runs a 3 s version (one seeded Zap Run with the max Loadout, through the house clear) in Vitest.
- **Ranking.** Each signed-in player appears once per board, with their best entry; anonymous entries are listed individually. `rank` in a POST reply is where that entry would sit in that list (a player's own better entry counts). Rate limits: anonymous posts per IP (6 Run / 9 Area entries per 10 min), signed-in posts per account (30 each). A repeat POST with the same `runId` returns the stored entry (200) instead of adding a row, so retries are safe.
- **Offline.** Without the API (404, non-JSON, 5xx, network error; a POST that times out after 10 s is a retryable error instead), every board falls back to localStorage (`dustbunny.leaderboard.local.v1` and `.local.areas.v1`).

## Accounts

Optional: the game works the same without them. Better Auth runs self-hosted in `api/auth.ts` with users and sessions in the same Neon database (tables `user`, `session`, `account`, `verification`). Neon's managed Auth was not used because it has no Discord sign-in. Providers: Google and Discord (`SOCIAL_PROVIDERS` in `src/shared/account.ts`); each is offered only when its client id and secret are set. Env vars are listed in `.env.example`.

- **Sign-in** opens the provider in a popup so the game keeps its state (an unsubmitted score survives). The popup lands on `public/auth-complete.html`, which tells the game over a `BroadcastChannel` and closes. When the popup is blocked, the page redirects and comes back to the same URL.
- **Verified scores**: `POST /api/leaderboard` reads the session cookie (`sessionUser`) and stores `user_id`; entries carry `verified`. The panel shows ✓ for verified and "anon" for anonymous names on the online board (none on the local board). Signed-in players' entries post automatically (see Leaderboard).
- **Cloud save** (`src/meta/cloudSave.ts`, `api/progress.ts`, table `player_progress`): Fluff, Upgrades, stats and `characters` (each Character's Hoard and bosses beaten; column `characters jsonb`, added to existing tables by `ensureSchema`). A body without `characters` (a bundle from before Keepsakes) keeps the stored Hoards and `keepsakesEarned`. Felting and shedding sync like spending: they change no lifetime counter and go through the dirty flag. On sign-in the copy with more lifetime Fluff earned (then more Runs) wins; guest progress is uploaded to an account that has less. Spending changes neither counter, so on a tie the device's copy wins only if it has changes the server never confirmed (`dust-bunny:cloud-dirty`, e.g. a purchase made offline); otherwise the server's copy wins, so a stale device can't undo another device's purchases. Later changes upload after 1.5 s; changes made while an upload is in flight upload next. The server refuses an upload with less lifetime progress and returns its copy, which the client adopts. `localStorage['dust-bunny:cloud-user']` remembers the last synced account, so signing in to another account on the same device loads that account's save instead of uploading the previous player's. `universes` (column `universes integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`) never goes down: the server stores the larger of the two counts even when it refuses an upload (a body without it, from before Act 9, keeps the stored count), the client keeps the larger count when it adopts the server's copy and takes the server's count after an accepted upload if that is higher; only loading another account's save replaces it. Settings, last Character, Difficulty, Loadout and `feltings` stay per device. The game runs on the client, so the server checks shape and bounds, not legitimacy.

## Debug hooks

URL params: `?debug`, `?seed=<string>` (same seed → same house, neighborhood, city and initial Debris), `?character=lint|tumble|zap` (otherwise the last chosen Character), `?difficulty=casual|arcade|hardcore` (otherwise the last chosen Difficulty), `?autostart` (skip the title screen and character select; also turns the story off), `?area=neighborhood` (start outside with a house-cleared Bunny, r = 56), `?area=city` (start in the city with a neighborhood-cleared Bunny, r = 185), `?area=region` (start in the region with a city-cleared Bunny, r = 0.9 units = 9 m), `?area=continent` (start in the continent with an Incinerator-beaten Bunny of 42 m, which the arrival leap takes to 0.85 units = 8.5 km), `?area=globe` (start on the Globe with an Ice-Sheet-beaten Bunny of 44 km, which the second leap takes to 0.85 units = 850 km), `?area=solar_system` (start in the Solar System with an Orbital-Sweeper-beaten Bunny of 4,400 km, which the third leap takes to 0.85 units = 0.0085 AU), `?area=galaxy` (start in the Galaxy with a Sun-beaten Bunny of 0.044 AU, which the fourth leap takes to 0.85 units = 85 ly), `?area=universe` (start in the Universe with a Sagittarius-A*-beaten Bunny of 400 ly, which the fifth leap takes to 0.85 units = 8.5 Mly, in the black-hole form).

`?debug` and `?area=` Runs are unranked: no leaderboard entries and no Keepsakes. To exercise drops in a browser test, play a ranked Run (`/?autostart&seed=test`) and call the installed tracker from the page: `(await import('/src/meta/keepsakes.ts')).getKeepsakeTracker().bossDefeated('robovac_9000')` (Vite serves the same module instance the game uses).

With `?debug`:
- `window.__game`: the `Game` (`.run`, `.view`, `.events`, `.loop`, `.screens`…).
- `window.__game.debugTools`: `grow(factor)`, `setRadius(r)`, `nextTier()`, `setTimeScale(s)`, `advance(seconds)` (simulates immediately), `openExit()`, `enterNeighborhood()`, `enterCity()`, `enterRegion()`, `enterContinent()`, `enterGlobe()`, `enterSolarSystem()`, `enterGalaxy()`, `enterUniverse()`, `setBunnyForm('dust' | 'black_hole')`, `nextArea()`, `playEnding(from?)` (ends the Run as a victory without `runEnded`, so nothing is saved, and plays the ending from shot `from`, then the results; the screen's `data-shot` / `data-t` say where it is), `spawnHazard(id)` (`setRadius` takes world units, `nextTier` converts).
- Keys: `E` (or the right mouse button) uses the Ability, `G` grows the radius ×1.25 (`Shift+G` shrinks it), `T` jumps to the next Tier, `N` clears the current area and rolls on to the next, `]` / `[` doubles / halves sim speed.
- The HUD bottom-left shows fps, draw calls, Debris count, and radius.

For browser tests, load `/?debug&autostart&seed=test`, then use `__game.debugTools.nextTier()` ×4 to reach Tier 5. For the neighborhood, load `/?debug&autostart&seed=test&area=neighborhood`; for the city, `&area=city`; for the region, `&area=region`; for the continent, `&area=continent`; for the globe, `&area=globe`; for the solar system, `&area=solar_system`; for the galaxy, `&area=galaxy`; for the universe, `&area=universe`. `pnpm sim --area=neighborhood|city|region|continent|globe|solar_system|galaxy|universe` measures later-area pacing (the summary prints each Area's median Act time, entry → boss absorbed) and `--difficulty=arcade|hardcore` a harder Difficulty; in a normal sim Run the bot skips the walk to each exit once an area is cleared.

## Known gaps

- Fluff is awarded for quit Runs too (per the design formula), so very short Runs still earn ~10 Fluff.
- Walls and furniture are one static batch (~80-120 draw calls at Tier 5 with Hazards). Rugs are one mesh each.
- At Tier 5 the view covers most of the house; the spawner uses whatever floor is still off screen (mostly behind the camera), and Tier 5 growth also comes from absorbable Hazards and boss chips.
- Furniture without a Blender model (tv_stand, stove, nightstand, dresser, desk, sink_cabinet, console_table, shoe_rack) is still procedural.
- The first-kill Keepsake choice lives in memory until the reveal (or, if the Run ended first, the results screen) closes; reloading before then keeps the first offer (already in the Hoard).
- `stats.feltings`, which gates the first-Felting caption, is per device, so that caption can play once on each device.
- The Ice Sheet's charges stun only on a mountain range: `isMountainRange` in `src/hazards/iceSheet.ts` (the Continent's `continent_range` / `continent_massif` landmarks, see `mountainObstacleIds`).
