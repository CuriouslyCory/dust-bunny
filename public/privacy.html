Privacy

Your personal data is private. We don't sell anything, including your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

If you sign in with Google or Discord, we store your name, email address and profile picture so you can keep your scores and save across devices. Leaderboard entries show only the name you choose.

To keep you signed in and to stop leaderboard spam, we also keep your IP address and browser type with your session, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address with submitted scores. Without an account, your progress stays in your browser.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
