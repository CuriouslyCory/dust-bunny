# Dust Bunny — Domain Glossary

- **Bunny** — the player character: a fuzzy sphere of dust. Its size is its **radius**, in the current Area's **unit**; its **real radius** is in centimetres.
- **Form** — what the Bunny is made of, for its look: **dust** until it absorbs Sagittarius A*, then a **black hole** (a dark core, an accretion disc of lint, a lensing shimmer; the same eyes) for the rest of the Run.
- **Character** — one of three starting Bunny archetypes chosen before a Run (Lint, Tumble, Zap). Each has different base stats.
- **Difficulty** — how hard a Run is, chosen with the Character: **Casual** (default, the original balance), **Arcade** (faster, more frequent Hazards with quicker attacks) or **Hardcore** (much more of that, one heart fewer, fewer Pickups, no heal between Areas). Harder Difficulties pay more Fluff and leaderboard score.
- **Run** — one play session from spawn to death or victory (absorbing Sagittarius A*, the Galaxy's boss, while the Galaxy is the last Area built). Runs are procedurally generated from a seed.
- **Debris** — any floor object the Bunny can absorb once it is big enough (lint, staples, coins, socks, shoes, chairs…). Do not call it "food" or "loot".
- **Absorb** — the act of the Bunny picking up Debris (or a shrunken Hazard). Absorbed Debris sticks visibly to the Bunny and adds mass.
- **Absorb ratio** — the largest Debris size the Bunny can absorb, as a fraction of its radius (default 0.7).
- **Obstacle** — Debris or furniture too big to absorb; it blocks movement. Some Debris is an Obstacle until a Tier whatever its size (Jupiter, until Dust Star).
- **Area** — the world a Run is in: first **the House**, then **the Neighborhood** (the street outside, reached through the **front door** once the Robo-Vac 9000 is absorbed), then **the City** (reached through the **road barrier** at the end of the main street once the Street Sweeper is absorbed), then **the Region** (reached by the **highway on-ramp** once the Garbage Truck is absorbed), then **the Continent** (reached by the **motorway west** out of the Region once the Incinerator is absorbed), then **the Globe** (the Earth seen from orbit, reached by the **launch corridor** on the Continent's north coast once the Ice Sheet is absorbed), then **the Solar System** (the ecliptic round the Sun, reached by the **escape trajectory** over the Globe's North Pole once the Orbital Sweeper is absorbed), then **the Galaxy** (the galactic disc round Sagittarius A*, reached through the **heliopause** on the Solar System's north edge once the Sun is absorbed). Once an area's exit is open the HUD's **skip button** moves on without rolling to it. Outdoors, **zones** (yard, street, sidewalk, driveway, park; in the city avenue, pavement, plaza, parking, square; in the region farmland, highway, river, town, woods, landfill; in the continent plains, mountains, lakes, rivers, desert, tundra, ruins; on the globe oceans, shelves, landmasses, archipelagos, icecaps; in the solar system corona, inner_orbits, asteroid_belt, outer_orbits, kuiper_belt) take the place of rooms.
- **Wrap-around** — the Globe's map has no east or west edge: rolling off one side arrives on the other, round the planet, along the **seam** (the date line, kept open ocean). The poles are its only edges. A **lap** is one trip round.
- **Unit** — how many centimetres one world unit is in an Area: 1 up to the City, 10 m in the Region, 10 km in the Continent, 1,000 km on the Globe (read out in Earths, Earth radii), 0.01 AU in the Solar System (read out in AU), 100 ly in the Galaxy (read out in light-years). Entering an Area **re-bases** the Bunny into its unit, keeping its real size, so every Act starts at about 1 unit. Readouts climb the ladder cm, m, km and beyond.
- **Size leap** — growth that happens off screen between two Areas when keeping the real size would leave the Bunny far below 1 unit: the Bunny arrives in the Continent at its **arrival radius** (8.5 km) instead of the ≈ 40 m it left the Region at, on the Globe at 850 km (0.13 Earths) instead of ≈ 45 km, in the Solar System at 0.0085 AU instead of ≈ 4,500 km, and in the Galaxy at 85 ly instead of ≈ 0.045 AU. It is not growth: no Tier-up, nothing scored.
- **Tier** — a growth stage (Speck, Fluff, Tumbleweed, Dust Devil, Dust Titan in the House; Dust Storm, Dust Cyclone in the Neighborhood; Dust Hurricane, Dust Colossus in the City; Dust Sprawl, Dust Front in the Region; Dustmass, Dust Pangaea in the Continent; Dust Moon, Dust Planet on the Globe; Dust Giant, Dust Star in the Solar System; Dust Nebula, Dust Cluster in the Galaxy). Crossing a Tier threshold widens the camera view, unlocks new areas of the floor, and offers a Perk draft.
- **Hazard** — a roaming cleaning device or supply (Roomba, Lint Roller, Broom, Spray Bottle, Mop, Upright Vacuum, Robo-Vac 9000 boss; outdoors: Robotic Mower, Lawn Mower, Leaf Blower, Sprinkler, Street Sweeper boss; in the city: Floor Scrubber, Pressure Washer, Litter Vacuum, Street Flusher, Garbage Truck boss; in the region: Landfill Compactor, Crop Duster, River Barge, Snowplow, Water Bomber, Freight Train, Incinerator boss; in the continent, weather: Tornado, Glacier Tongue, Volcano, River Surge, Hurricane, Ice Sheet boss; on the globe: Debris-Field Satellite, Space Station, Anti-Satellite Laser, Re-entry Burn, Hurricane, Orbital Sweeper boss; in the solar system: Rogue Asteroid, Comet, Sunspot, Sungrazer, Solar Flare, the Sun boss; in the galaxy: Black Hole, Globular Cluster, Pulsar, Supernova Remnant, Supernova, Sagittarius A* boss) that damages the Bunny. Hazards small enough relative to the Bunny can be absorbed.
- **Health** — whole hit points shown as hearts. Reaching zero ends the Run.
- **Pickup** — a temporary, timed power-up found on the floor during a Run (Static Charge, Sugar Rush, Bubble Wrap, Glue Dab, Crumb Cake, Dust Cloud).
- **Perk** — a run-long upgrade chosen from a draft of three at each Tier-up. Lost when the Run ends.
- **Upgrade** — a permanent meta-progression improvement bought in the Shop with Fluff. Persists across Runs.
- **Fluff** — the meta currency earned at the end of each Run.
- **Account** — optional sign-in with Google or Discord. A signed-in player's Fluff, Upgrades and stats follow them between devices (the **cloud save**).
- **Verified score** — a leaderboard entry posted while signed in, shown with a ✓. Entries posted without an account are **anonymous**.
- **Run board** / **Area board** — the leaderboards. The Run board ranks whole Runs; each Area has an Area board that takes an entry whenever a Run clears that Area. Signed-in players' entries post automatically; anonymous players choose to submit them on the results screen.
- **Dash** — a short speed burst on a cooldown.
- **Ability** — each Character's own move, unlocked from the first Run and used with E, the right mouse button or the touch Ability button: Lint's **Decoy**, Tumble's **Hunker**, Zap's **Discharge**. An Ability can stun a boss at most once every 6 s, and every chip still needs contact.
- **Decoy** — Lint's Ability (tap): a lint ball shed on the floor for a few seconds. Hazards that aim at the Bunny aim at it instead while it is in their range, and a Hazard that touches it is **clogged** (briefly stunned). Sprays and blasts pop it.
- **Hunker** — Tumble's Ability (hold): Tumble plants itself. While planted it can't move, takes no damage, sheds no mass and can't be pushed, pulled or stuck; bosses crash into it as into a wall. The cooldown grows with the time held.
- **Discharge** — Zap's Ability (tap when the Static meter is full): shorts out the nearest Hazard in range. A normal Hazard is destroyed into scrap; a boss is stunned (not chipped) and then **Grounded**.
- **Static meter** — Zap's charge for Discharge: it fills while Zap rolls fast (Dash counts double), drains while Zap is slow, and shows as Zap's glow and the HUD ring.
- **Grounded** — a boss that was just Discharged; it can't be Discharged again for 10 s.
- **Cap** — a per-Character limit on a stat (for example, Tumble's speed never exceeds 1.05) applied after Shop Upgrades, Keepsakes and Perks but before timed effects such as Pickups and Hazard slows. The Perk draft stops offering a Perk whose stats are all at cap. The **envelope** is the most a stat can reach permanently (base + maxed Shop + the best two Keepsakes).
- **Keepsake** — an Ability modifier a boss leaves tangled in the fur of the Character that absorbed it. Five types per Character, usable only by that Character. Shown as type plus rarity, e.g. "Thick Clog · Epic". Not "loot" or "item".
- **Rarity** — a Keepsake's strength: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary or Mythic (1–5). Rolled on each drop from the boss's **drop table**, which favours higher rarities the later the boss (the Robo-Vac 9000 is 84% Common, the Ice Sheet 74% Epic, the Expansion 84% Mythic). A boss's most likely rarity is its **peak**. Replaces the old **grade** (1–10, one per boss), which saves and leaderboards from before are read as, two grades to a rarity.
- **Hoard** — one Character's Keepsakes, counted by type and rarity (at most 99 of each). Kept in the save and the cloud save.
- **Loadout** — the 0–2 Keepsakes tucked into a Character's fur for a Run; fixed for the Run and remembered per Character on the device.
- **Tuck in** — the UI verb for putting a Keepsake into a Loadout slot.
- **Felting** — turning 3 Keepsakes of the same type and rarity into 1 of the next rarity, for Fluff, up to one rarity above the peak of the best boss that Character has beaten. **Shedding** discards one for a little Fluff.
- **Under the Couch** — the Bunny's den and hub screen where each Character's Hoard is shown and felted.
- **Story** — the deadpan narrator's storyline (`docs/story.md`), told in short captions on Tier-ups, boss arrivals, Area changes and results. Each **Area** is a **Chapter** with a title (the Neighborhood is Chapter 2, *The Front Door Was Built for People*). Silenced by the **Story captions** setting.
